🎯 How to Study Effectively

  1. Understand the concept before memorising facts.
  2. Make short notes and revise them weekly.
  3. Practise questions topic by topic.
  4. Review every incorrect answer.
  5. Use official exam notifications for dates and rules.
Resources on this website are educational support materials. Always verify exam-specific syllabus and notices from the relevant official authority.
